Profile

He spent his career inside one of the largest city administrations in the world. Now he puts that experience to work for the environment.

As Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the BMC's 'B' Ward, Udaykumar Shiroorkar took on unauthorised construction and reclaimed public space in Madanpura, Pydhonie, Abdul Rehman Street and Nagpada.

He is best known for something quieter. He pushed the ward's Class IV civic workers, many of them sweepers and manhole cleaners earning under Rs 6,000 a month, to return to their studies.

Twenty-three of them passed their SSC exams, years after leaving school. The recognition that followed persuaded 26 more to join night classes.

Today his focus is the environment and civic responsibility, which he documents in short Instagram Reels for a growing audience.

Source: Free Press Journal, April 2025

Media and film

From the ward to the screen.

The story of the civic workers he encouraged back to school became the Marathi feature film Ata Thambaycha Naay! ("Now we won't stop"), released on 1 May 2025. Ashutosh Gowariker plays him. The film won Best Film at the Filmfare Awards Marathi.
