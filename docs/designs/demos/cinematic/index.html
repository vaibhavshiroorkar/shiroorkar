A true story . Mumbai

Now, wewon't stop.

Udaykumar Shiroorkar spent his career in Mumbai's civic administration. One quiet decision of his became a feature film. Today he works for the environment and for the people who keep a city running.

IThe ward

Some of the densest streets in Mumbai.

As Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the BMC's 'B' Ward, he took on unauthorised construction in Madanpura, Pydhonie, Abdul Rehman Street and Nagpada, and gave public space back to the public.

Source: Free Press Journal, April 2025

IIThe workers

He asked the people who clean the city to go back to school.

Sweepers, manhole cleaners, road and pipeline repairmen. Many earned under Rs 6,000 a month and had left school early. He encouraged them to study again.

23

passed their SSC exams, years after leaving school.

26

more joined night classes once they saw it could be done.

1

feature film followed.

Source: Free Press Journal, April 2025

"The recognition they received from society had a ripple effect."Udaykumar Shiroorkar

IIIThe film

Ata Thambaycha Naay!

The Marathi feature film based on his story opened in theatres on 1 May 2025. Shivraj Waichal directed it. Ashutosh Gowariker plays Udaykumar. It won Best Film at the Filmfare Awards Marathi.

No poster or film still is shown here without the producers' permission.
