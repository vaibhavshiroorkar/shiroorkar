01

Summary

PHOTO: in the field, 3:4

As Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the BMC's 'B' Ward, he took on unauthorised construction and reclaimed public space in Madanpura, Pydhonie, Abdul Rehman Street and Nagpada.1

He also pushed the ward's Class IV civic workers back to their studies. Twenty-three passed their SSC exams; 26 more then joined night classes.1

Today he works on environmental and civic causes and shares that work on Instagram.
