Retired BMC Assistant Municipal Commissioner. The true story behind Ata Thambaycha Naay!

In his words

ABP Live . Lead Inc. series

Service Beyond Duty: In Conversation With Udaykumar Shiroorkar

Watch on YouTube (opens in a new tab)

1 / 5

Second chances

He asked the people who clean Mumbai to go back to school. Twenty-three of them passed.

1 of 4

The school

The Dongri Municipal Night School was about to close for lack of students. He saw a way to keep it open.

Nagpur Today(opens in a new tab)

2 of 4

The workers

Sweepers, manhole cleaners and repairmen aged 30 to 50, many earning under Rs 6,000 a month. He filled in their forms himself.

YourStory, June 2017(opens in a new tab)

3 of 4

The result

In June 2017, 21 men and 2 women passed their SSC exams and earned raises of up to Rs 3,000. Then 26 more joined night classes.

YourStory, June 2017(opens in a new tab)

4 of 4

The film

Their story became the Marathi feature film Ata Thambaycha Naay!, released on 1 May 2025, with Ashutosh Gowariker in his role.

Wikipedia: Ata Thambaycha Naay!(opens in a new tab)

Public space

In some of Mumbai's densest streets, he gave public space back to the public.

1 of 4

B Ward

As Assistant Municipal Commissioner of 'B' Ward he was responsible for Madanpura, Pydhonie, Abdul Rehman Street and Nagpada.

Free Press Journal, April 2025(opens in a new tab)

2 of 4

The drives

He took a firm stand against unauthorised construction and led demolition drives to reclaim public space.

Free Press Journal, April 2025(opens in a new tab)

3 of 4

Two wards

In 2018 he ran B Ward and C Ward at the same time, and moved to demolish illegal buildings on Ismail Curtay Street.

DNA India, May 2018(opens in a new tab)

4 of 4

Singham

That firmness earned him a name across Mumbai: the BMC's "Singham".

Prahaar, May 2025 (Marathi)(opens in a new tab)

Clean city

He wanted every call to the ward office to carry a reason to keep Mumbai clean.

1 of 3

The tune

In 2017 he planned a Swachh Mumbai jingle as the caller tune for B Ward's civic helpline.

DNA India, August 2017(opens in a new tab)

2 of 3

The message

Its words would change with each campaign: waste segregation one month, the work of street-cleaning staff the next.

DNA India, August 2017(opens in a new tab)

3 of 3

The videos

Short videos on the ward's cleanliness work were planned for residents as well.

DNA India, August 2017(opens in a new tab)
